03

Selection — convert or disqualify

Lead conversion → Opportunity opens in Discovery
Pillar 01Pillar 02

You do

  • Convert a qualified lead — creates Account, Contact and Opportunity in one action
  • Or disqualify with a reason, which stays on the lead permanently
  • In Discovery: document the pain point and set Business Qualification

What the system does

  • Carries source attribution onto the new opportunity
  • Opens the opportunity at Discovery, 15%, and stamps the stage entry date
  • Blocks the exit from Discovery until Business Qualification is Qualified and the pain point is filled

Watch out

  • Closed Lost is always available from Discovery — the gate only applies to moving forward

04

Commit — the two gates and the knot

Technical Validation → Proof of Concept → Negotiations → Security Review
Pillar 02Pillar 05Pillar 06

You do

  • AE: establish business fit, keep Next Step current, negotiate and submit for approval
  • SE: establish technical fit — autonomy level, coding agents, IDEs, incumbent SAST/SCA stack
  • Record the POC scoping link, summary and explicit success criteria

What the system does

  • Refuses entry to Proof of Concept unless both gates are Qualified, BANT is 75% or more, and amount, close date, champion and pain point are all present
  • Stamps the POC start date on stage entry — days in stage is the POC clock
  • Refuses entry to Security Review until the CEO has approved the value
  • Notifies the team when a POC starts

Watch out

  • A technical loss does not close the deal — deals are still won on commercial grounds
  • Only Confirmed counts toward BANT. Estimated and Partial score zero

Click path — prove the gate

  1. Open Northwind Labs — BANT reads 50%, Technical Qualification Not Yet Assessed
  2. Try to move the stage to Proof of Concept
  3. Salesforce refuses, naming exactly what is missing
  4. Then open Aperture Systems — BANT 75%, both gates Qualified — and move it. It goes through
Open Northwind Labs → Open Aperture Systems →
05

Onboard — the win, and what it triggers

Closed Won → renewal created automatically
Pillar 05

You do

  • Capture contract start, end, term, payment terms and billing frequency
  • Move the stage to Closed Won and record why you won

What the system does

  • Creates the renewal opportunity from the contract term, dated to the day after this one ends
  • Clones every product line onto it, re-dated to the renewal term
  • Links it back with Renewal_Of__c
  • Fires the win alert and the customer-onboarding request

Grow — expansion and co-terming

Expansion record type · Co-Term with Renewal action
Pillar 05

You do

  • Raise an Expansion opportunity on an existing customer
  • Use Co-Term with Renewal to fold it into the open renewal

What the system does

The six workflows that carry the process

Everything above rests on these. If a demo only has time for a handful, take the two marked as the ones people react to.

Show this one · Pillar 05

Closed Won creates the renewal

Moving to Closed Won generates the renewal opportunity from the contract term, clones every product line onto it re-dated to the renewal period, links the two records, and fires the win and onboarding alerts. Verified live: Basalt Security won at $88,000 produced a 2027 renewal carrying both line items.

Verified end to endOnboardGrow

Show this one · Pillar 05

Expansion co-terming

A guided action lists open renewals on the account, mirrors the expansion's line items onto the chosen one at the renewal's dates, and lets the total recalculate natively. Co-terming by hand is where expansion revenue is most often mis-dated.

Verified end to endGrow

Pillar 02 & 06

Two-gate qualification with a 75% BANT threshold

Business and technical qualification are assessed independently by different people and both must be Qualified. BANT scores only Confirmed answers; three of four is the bar. The business gate is re-checked at POC entry so a deal cannot slip through after leaving Discovery.

Gate tested both waysSelectionCommit

Pillar 06

CEO pricing approval

The negotiated value routes to the CEO. While it is pending, the move into Security Review is refused. Approval unlocks the move — it does not make it, because the rep should still decide when the deal is ready.

Submit, block, approve testedCommit

Pillar 03

Activity recency, maintained by the system

Every logged call, email and meeting rolls onto the contact or lead and up to the account. Sales and marketing are tracked separately so a marketing send cannot make a dormant account look active.

31 activities seededEducationImpact

Pillar 01 & 05

Stage and next-step stamping

Stage entry date is stamped on every change, giving days-in-stage and the POC clock for free. The next-step date is stamped whenever it changes, so a deal untouched for a week flags itself on a list view rather than waiting for someone to notice.
